Reading this post from Online Goddess, and obviously got me thinking, with all the time and effort for building and using this blog as my medium for documenting my journey about something, tech, project, life, or other thing.

Sometimes sharing things that interest me, and might be useful for other people.

The actual thing that cost me money, currently, only the domain, I have bunch of it. I bought this domain for sometimes, some of this blog post has survived few provider, and ended up using Jekyll as my engine, it's simple, works with my workflow, GitHub -> Netlify and that's it.

Other post might have other view the cost of being online, [here](https://anarchaeopteryx.bearblog.dev/2026-11-07-cost-of-being-online/) and [here](https://olu.online/the-cost-of-being-online/).

Even though I and not counting those cost, but the mental model, cognitive load, and cognitive exercise are huge benefit for me, in this AI era. Yes, sometimes I missed the struggle of thinking, hit the wall, or can't find the exact word for things I'm thinking of. Now, just throw it to AI, and [it will figure it out](/2026/06/rediscovering-software-engineering-passion), one way or another, true answer, [sloppy or hallucinating](/2026/08/speed-trap).

I do like [writing](/2026/07/slowing-down) (and reading), even though sometimes falls into "*summarize this book for me*" trap, with reading without asking AI, we can see inside the brain of the author.
